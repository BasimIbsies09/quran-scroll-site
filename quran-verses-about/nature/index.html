Quran Verses About Nature and Creation

The Quran constantly invites you to look: at the sky, the mountains, the rain and the night. These ayahs are signs in creation.

  1. Ali 'Imran 3:190

    إِنَّ فِى خَلْقِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ وَٱخْتِلَٰفِ ٱلَّيْلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِ لَءَايَٰتٍۢ لِّأُو۟لِى ٱلْأَلْبَٰبِ

    Inna fee khalqis samaawati wal ardi wakhtilaafil laili wannahaari la Aayaatil liulil albaab

    Indeed, in the creation of the heavens and the earth and the alternation of the night and the day are signs for those of understanding.

    Why it speaks to thisThis ayah invites you to see the heavens, the earth, and the turning of night and day as signs. Your reflection is exactly what it points to.

  2. Ali 'Imran 3:191

    ٱلَّذِينَ يَذْكُرُونَ ٱللَّهَ قِيَٰمًۭا وَقُعُودًۭا وَعَلَىٰ جُنُوبِهِمْ وَيَتَفَكَّرُونَ فِى خَلْقِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ رَبَّنَا مَا خَلَقْتَ هَٰذَا بَٰطِلًۭا سُبْحَٰنَكَ فَقِنَا عَذَابَ ٱلنَّارِ

    Allazeena yazkuroonal laaha qiyaamaiw-wa qu'oodanw-wa 'alaa juno obihim wa yatafakkaroona fee khalqis samaawaati wal ardi Rabbanaa maa khalaqta haaza baatilan Subhaanak faqinaa 'azaaban Naar

    Who remember Allah while standing or sitting or [lying] on their sides and give thought to the creation of the heavens and the earth, [saying], "Our Lord, You did not create this aimlessly; exalted are You [above such a thing]; then protect us from the punishment of the Fire.

    Why it speaks to thisThose who reflect on creation come to feel its purpose and respond in remembrance and humility. May your contemplation lead you there too.

  3. Ar-Rum 30:22

    وَمِنْ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ خَلْقُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ وَٱخْتِلَٰفُ أَلْسِنَتِكُمْ وَأَلْوَٰنِكُمْ ۚ إِنَّ فِى ذَٰلِكَ لَءَايَٰتٍۢ لِّلْعَٰلِمِينَ

    Wa min Aayaatihee khalqus samaawaati wal aardi wakhtilaafu alsinatikum wa alwaanikum; inna fee zaalika la Aayaatil lil'aalimeen

    And of His signs is the creation of the heavens and the earth and the diversity of your languages and your colors. Indeed in that are signs for those of knowledge.

    Why it speaks to thisEven the diversity among people is named among Allah's signs. You can find wonder in the sky above and in the faces around you.

  4. Ar-Rahman 55:5

    ٱلشَّمْسُ وَٱلْقَمَرُ بِحُسْبَانٍۢ

    Ashshamsu walqamaru bihusbaan

    The sun and the moon [move] by precise calculation,

    Why it speaks to thisThe steady order of the sun and moon is a quiet reminder of care and design. Watching the sky can be a way into reflection.

  5. An-Nahl 16:10

    هُوَ ٱلَّذِىٓ أَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءًۭ ۖ لَّكُم مِّنْهُ شَرَابٌۭ وَمِنْهُ شَجَرٌۭ فِيهِ تُسِيمُونَ

    Huwal lazeee anzala minas samaaa'i maaa'al lakum minhu sharaabunw wa minhu shajarun feehi tuseemoon

    It is He who sends down rain from the sky; from it is drink and from it is foliage in which you pasture [animals].

    Why it speaks to thisRain, drink, and growing things are all gifts. Next time you watch rain fall or plants grow, this ayah can deepen your gratitude.

  6. Al-Ghashiyah 88:17

    أَفَلَا يَنظُرُونَ إِلَى ٱلْإِبِلِ كَيْفَ خُلِقَتْ

    Afalaa yanzuroona ilalibili kaifa khuliqat

    Then do they not look at the camels - how they are created?

    Why it speaks to thisThe Quran asks us to look closely at the creatures around us. Taking in an animal's design can be a form of reflection.

  7. Adh-Dhariyat 51:20

    وَفِى ٱلْأَرْضِ ءَايَٰتٌۭ لِّلْمُوقِنِينَ

    Wa fil ardi aayaatul lilmooqineen

    And on the earth are signs for the certain [in faith]

    Why it speaks to thisThe ground beneath your feet carries signs for those who seek certainty. Walking outside with open eyes can be a quiet act of reflection.

  8. Al-A'raf 7:54

    إِنَّ رَبَّكُمُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضَ فِى سِتَّةِ أَيَّامٍۢ ثُمَّ ٱسْتَوَىٰ عَلَى ٱلْعَرْشِ يُغْشِى ٱلَّيْلَ ٱلنَّهَارَ يَطْلُبُهُۥ حَثِيثًۭا وَٱلشَّمْسَ وَٱلْقَمَرَ وَٱلنُّجُومَ مُسَخَّرَٰتٍۭ بِأَمْرِهِۦٓ ۗ أَلَا لَهُ ٱلْخَلْقُ وَٱلْأَمْرُ ۗ تَبَارَكَ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ

    Inna Rabbakkumul laahul lazee khalaqas sammaawaati wal arda fee sittati qiyaamin summmas tawaa 'alal 'arshi yughshil lailan nahaara yatlu buhoo haseesanw washshamsa walqamara wannujooma musakhkharaatim bi amrih; alaa lahul khalqu wal-amr; tabaarakal laahu Rabbul 'aalameen

    Indeed, your Lord is Allah, who created the heavens and earth in six days and then established Himself above the Throne. He covers the night with the day, [another night] chasing it rapidly; and [He created] the sun, the moon, and the stars, subjected by His command. Unquestionably, His is the creation and the command; blessed is Allah, Lord of the worlds.

    Why it speaks to thisThis ayah reminds you of the Creator's majesty behind all that you see. It's a good place to rest your heart as you reflect.
