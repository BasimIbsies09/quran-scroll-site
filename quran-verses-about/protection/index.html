Quran Verses About Protection

For seeking Allah's protection: the ayahs and surahs Muslims recite for refuge from harm, envy and whispers.

  1. Al-Falaq 113:1

    قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلْفَلَقِ

    Qul a'uzoo bi rabbil-falaq

    Say, "I seek refuge in the Lord of daybreak

    Why it speaks to thisThis surah begins by placing your trust in the Lord of daybreak. You can turn to Him for refuge from every harm you fear.

  2. Al-Falaq 113:5

    وَمِن شَرِّ حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ

    Wa min shar ri haasidin iza hasad

    And from the evil of an envier when he envies."

    Why it speaks to thisThese words name envy directly, which is often linked with the fear of the evil eye. You are taught to seek refuge in Allah from it.

  3. An-Nas 114:1

    قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلنَّاسِ

    Qul a'uzu birabbin naas

    Say, "I seek refuge in the Lord of mankind,

    Why it speaks to thisYou are invited to take shelter in the Lord of all people. His protection is greater than any harm that might reach you.

  4. Al-Baqarah 2:255

    ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلْحَىُّ ٱلْقَيُّومُ ۚ لَا تَأْخُذُهُۥ سِنَةٌۭ وَلَا نَوْمٌۭ ۚ لَّهُۥ مَا فِى ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ ۗ مَن ذَا ٱلَّذِى يَشْفَعُ عِندَهُۥٓ إِلَّا بِإِذْنِهِۦ ۚ يَعْلَمُ مَا بَيْنَ أَيْدِيهِمْ وَمَا خَلْفَهُمْ ۖ وَلَا يُحِيطُونَ بِشَىْءٍۢ مِّنْ عِلْمِهِۦٓ إِلَّا بِمَا شَآءَ ۚ وَسِعَ كُرْسِيُّهُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضَ ۖ وَلَا يَـُٔودُهُۥ حِفْظُهُمَا ۚ وَهُوَ ٱلْعَلِىُّ ٱلْعَظِيمُ

    Allahu laaa ilaaha illaa Huwal Haiyul Qaiyoom; laa taakhuzuhoo sinatunw wa laa nawm; lahoo maa fissamaawaati wa maa fil ard; man zal lazee yashfa'u indahooo illaa bi-iznih; ya'lamu maa baina aydeehim wa mww khalfahum wa laa yuheetoona bishai'im min 'ilmihee illaa bimaa shaaa'; wasi'a Kursiyyuhus samaawaati wal arda wa laa ya'ooduho hifzuhumaa; wa Huwal Aliyyul 'Azeem

    Allah - there is no deity except Him, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer of [all] existence. Neither drowsiness overtakes Him nor sleep. To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth. Who is it that can intercede with Him except by His permission? He knows what is [presently] before them and what will be after them, and they encompass not a thing of His knowledge except for what He wills. His Kursi extends over the heavens and the earth, and their preservation tires Him not. And He is the Most High, the Most Great.

    Why it speaks to thisAyat al-Kursi reminds you that Allah is Ever-Living and watchful. Many readers recite it to feel safe and to recall His care.

  5. At-Tawbah 9:51

    قُل لَّن يُصِيبَنَآ إِلَّا مَا كَتَبَ ٱللَّهُ لَنَا هُوَ مَوْلَىٰنَا ۚ وَعَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَلْيَتَوَكَّلِ ٱلْمُؤْمِنُونَ

    Qul lany-yuseebanaaa illaa maa katabal laahu lanaa Huwa mawlaanaa; wa 'alal laahi falyatawak kalimu 'minoon

    Say, "Never will we be struck except by what Allah has decreed for us; He is our protector." And upon Allah let the believers rely.

    Why it speaks to thisThis ayah can settle your heart with trust that everything is in Allah's hands. You can rely on Him with calm.

  6. At-Talaq 65:3

    وَيَرْزُقْهُ مِنْ حَيْثُ لَا يَحْتَسِبُ ۚ وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسْبُهُۥٓ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بَٰلِغُ أَمْرِهِۦ ۚ قَدْ جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ لِكُلِّ شَىْءٍۢ قَدْرًۭا

    Wa yarzuqhu min haisu laa yahtasib; wa many yatawakkal 'alal laahi fahuwa husbuh; innal laaha baalighu amrih; qad ja'alal laahu likulli shai'in qadraa

    And will provide for him from where he does not expect. And whoever relies upon Allah - then He is sufficient for him. Indeed, Allah will accomplish His purpose. Allah has already set for everything a [decreed] extent.

    Why it speaks to thisWhen you place your trust in Allah, He is enough for you. Let that bring you reassurance and peace.

  7. Al-Mu'minun 23:97

    وَقُل رَّبِّ أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ هَمَزَٰتِ ٱلشَّيَٰطِينِ

    Wa qur Rabbi a'oozu bika min hamazaatish Shayaateen

    And say, "My Lord, I seek refuge in You from the incitements of the devils,

    Why it speaks to thisThis is a du'a you can make in your own words, asking Allah to keep you safe from the whispers and harm of the devils.

  8. Yusuf 12:64

    قَالَ هَلْ ءَامَنُكُمْ عَلَيْهِ إِلَّا كَمَآ أَمِنتُكُمْ عَلَىٰٓ أَخِيهِ مِن قَبْلُ ۖ فَٱللَّهُ خَيْرٌ حَٰفِظًۭا ۖ وَهُوَ أَرْحَمُ ٱلرَّٰحِمِينَ

    Qaala hal aamanukum 'alihi illaa kamaa amintukum 'alaaa akheehimin qabl; fal laahu khairun haafizanw wa Huwa arhamur Raahimeen

    He said, "Should I entrust you with him except [under coercion] as I entrusted you with his brother before? But Allah is the best guardian, and He is the most merciful of the merciful."

    Why it speaks to thisThese words remind you that Allah is the best of protectors and full of mercy. You can entrust yourself and your loved ones to Him.
