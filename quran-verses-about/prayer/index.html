Quran Verses About Prayer (Salah)

For anyone working on their salah. These ayahs speak of prayer, its times and the humility of those who pray.

  1. Taha 20:14

    إِنَّنِىٓ أَنَا ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّآ أَنَا۠ فَٱعْبُدْنِى وَأَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ لِذِكْرِىٓ

    Innaneee Anal laahu laaa ilaaha illaa Ana fa'budnee wa aqimis-salaata lizikree

    Indeed, I am Allah. There is no deity except Me, so worship Me and establish prayer for My remembrance.

    Why it speaks to thisAllah describes prayer as a way to remember Him. You can come to salah simply as a meeting of remembrance, however you feel that day.

  2. Al-Baqarah 2:45

    وَٱسْتَعِينُوا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ وَٱلصَّلَوٰةِ ۚ وَإِنَّهَا لَكَبِيرَةٌ إِلَّا عَلَى ٱلْخَٰشِعِينَ

    Wasta'eenoo bissabri was Salaah; wa innahaa lakabee ratun illaa alal khaashi'een

    And seek help through patience and prayer, and indeed, it is difficult except for the humbly submissive [to Allah]

    Why it speaks to thisConsistency is something you can ask help for. Patience and prayer are named together as sources of support on this path.

  3. Al-'Ankabut 29:45

    ٱتْلُ مَآ أُوحِىَ إِلَيْكَ مِنَ ٱلْكِتَٰبِ وَأَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ ۖ إِنَّ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ تَنْهَىٰ عَنِ ٱلْفَحْشَآءِ وَٱلْمُنكَرِ ۗ وَلَذِكْرُ ٱللَّهِ أَكْبَرُ ۗ وَٱللَّهُ يَعْلَمُ مَا تَصْنَعُونَ

    Utlu maaa oohiya ilaika mional Kitaabi wa aqimis Salaata innas Salaata tanhaa 'anil fahshaaa'i wal munkar; wa lazikrul laahi akbar; wal laahu ya'lamu maa tasna'oon

    Recite, [O Muhammad], what has been revealed to you of the Book and establish prayer. Indeed, prayer prohibits immorality and wrongdoing, and the remembrance of Allah is greater. And Allah knows that which you do.

    Why it speaks to thisThis ayah shows the lasting good prayer can bring into your life, which can encourage you to keep returning to it.

  4. Ibrahim 14:40

    رَبِّ ٱجْعَلْنِى مُقِيمَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةِ وَمِن ذُرِّيَّتِى ۚ رَبَّنَا وَتَقَبَّلْ دُعَآءِ

    Rabbij 'alnee muqeemas Salaati wa min zurriyyatee Rabbanaa wa taqabbal du'aaa'

    My Lord, make me an establisher of prayer, and [many] from my descendants. Our Lord, and accept my supplication.

    Why it speaks to thisThis is a du'a of Ibrahim that you can make yourself, asking Allah to help you stay steady in salah.

  5. Al-Baqarah 2:186

    وَإِذَا سَأَلَكَ عِبَادِى عَنِّى فَإِنِّى قَرِيبٌ ۖ أُجِيبُ دَعْوَةَ ٱلدَّاعِ إِذَا دَعَانِ ۖ فَلْيَسْتَجِيبُوا۟ لِى وَلْيُؤْمِنُوا۟ بِى لَعَلَّهُمْ يَرْشُدُونَ

    Wa izaa sa alaka 'ibaadee 'annnee fa innee qareebun ujeebu da'wataddaa'i izaa da'aani falyastajeeboo lee walyu minoo beela 'allahum yarshudoon

    And when My servants ask you, [O Muhammad], concerning Me - indeed I am near. I respond to the invocation of the supplicant when he calls upon Me. So let them respond to Me [by obedience] and believe in Me that they may be [rightly] guided.

    Why it speaks to thisAllah tells you He is near and answers when you call. You can bring your wish for closeness directly to Him.

  6. Al-Baqarah 2:238

    حَٰفِظُوا۟ عَلَى ٱلصَّلَوَٰتِ وَٱلصَّلَوٰةِ ٱلْوُسْطَىٰ وَقُومُوا۟ لِلَّهِ قَٰنِتِينَ

    Haafizoo 'alas salawaati was Salaatil Wustaa wa qoomoo lillaahi qaaniteen

    Maintain with care the [obligatory] prayers and [in particular] the middle prayer and stand before Allah, devoutly obedient.

    Why it speaks to thisYou are encouraged to guard your prayers with care. Small, steady efforts to protect your salah are part of this call.

  7. Ar-Ra'd 13:28

    ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَتَطْمَئِنُّ قُلُوبُهُم بِذِكْرِ ٱللَّهِ ۗ أَلَا بِذِكْرِ ٱللَّهِ تَطْمَئِنُّ ٱلْقُلُوبُ

    Allazeena aamanoo wa tatma'innu quloobuhum bizikril laah; alaa bizikril laahi tatma'innul quloob

    Those who have believed and whose hearts are assured by the remembrance of Allah. Unquestionably, by the remembrance of Allah hearts are assured."

    Why it speaks to thisRemembering Allah brings hearts calm and reassurance. May your salah become a place where your heart finds rest.
