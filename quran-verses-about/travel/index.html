Quran Verses About Travel

For journeys. These ayahs include the du'a of travel and reminders that the earth is Allah's.

  1. Az-Zukhruf 43:13

    لِتَسْتَوُۥا۟ عَلَىٰ ظُهُورِهِۦ ثُمَّ تَذْكُرُوا۟ نِعْمَةَ رَبِّكُمْ إِذَا ٱسْتَوَيْتُمْ عَلَيْهِ وَتَقُولُوا۟ سُبْحَٰنَ ٱلَّذِى سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَٰذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُۥ مُقْرِنِينَ

    Litastawoo 'alaa zuhoorihee summa tazkuroo ni'mata Rabbikum izastawaitum 'alaihi wa taqooloo Subhaanal lazee sakhkhara lana haaza wa maa kunnaa lahoo muqrineen

    That you may settle yourselves upon their backs and then remember the favor of your Lord when you have settled upon them and say. "Exalted is He who has subjected this to us, and we could not have [otherwise] subdued it.

    Why it speaks to thisThese words are the well-known du'a for riding or traveling. They remind you to remember Allah's favor in every means of transport you use.

  2. Az-Zukhruf 43:14

    وَإِنَّآ إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا لَمُنقَلِبُونَ

    Wa innaaa ilaa Rabbinaa lamunqaliboon

    And indeed we, to our Lord, will [surely] return."

    Why it speaks to thisThis continues the travel du'a, a gentle reminder that every journey ends with a return to your Lord.

  3. Hud 11:41

    ۞ وَقَالَ ٱرْكَبُوا۟ فِيهَا بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ مَجْر۪ىٰهَا وَمُرْسَىٰهَآ ۚ إِنَّ رَبِّى لَغَفُورٌۭ رَّحِيمٌۭ

    Wa qaalar kaboo feehaa bismil laahi majraihaa wa mursaahaa; inna Rabbee la Ghafoorur Raheem

    And [Noah] said, "Embark therein; in the name of Allah is its course and its anchorage. Indeed, my Lord is Forgiving and Merciful."

    Why it speaks to thisSpoken as the ark set sail, these words place both the departure and the arrival in Allah's name. You can begin your journey the same way.

  4. Al-Mu'minun 23:29

    وَقُل رَّبِّ أَنزِلْنِى مُنزَلًۭا مُّبَارَكًۭا وَأَنتَ خَيْرُ ٱلْمُنزِلِينَ

    Wa qur Rabbi anzilnee munzalam mubaarakanw wa Anta khairul munzileen

    And say, 'My Lord, let me land at a blessed landing place, and You are the best to accommodate [us].' "

    Why it speaks to thisA du'a to ask for a blessed arrival. You can make it as you head toward your destination.

  5. Al-Isra 17:80

    وَقُل رَّبِّ أَدْخِلْنِى مُدْخَلَ صِدْقٍۢ وَأَخْرِجْنِى مُخْرَجَ صِدْقٍۢ وَٱجْعَل لِّى مِن لَّدُنكَ سُلْطَٰنًۭا نَّصِيرًۭا

    Wa qur Rabbi adkhilnee mudkhala sidqinw wa akhrijnee mukhraja sidqinw waj'al lee milladunka sultaanan naseeraa

    And say, "My Lord, cause me to enter a sound entrance and to exit a sound exit and grant me from Yourself a supporting authority."

    Why it speaks to thisThis du'a asks for goodness in leaving and in arriving, which suits the start of any journey.

  6. Ali 'Imran 3:173

    ٱلَّذِينَ قَالَ لَهُمُ ٱلنَّاسُ إِنَّ ٱلنَّاسَ قَدْ جَمَعُوا۟ لَكُمْ فَٱخْشَوْهُمْ فَزَادَهُمْ إِيمَٰنًۭا وَقَالُوا۟ حَسْبُنَا ٱللَّهُ وَنِعْمَ ٱلْوَكِيلُ

    Allazeena qaala lahumun naasu innan naasa qad jama'oo lakum fakhshawhum fazaadahum eemaannanwa wa qaaloo hasbunal laahu wa ni'malwakeel

    Those to whom hypocrites said, "Indeed, the people have gathered against you, so fear them." But it [merely] increased them in faith, and they said, "Sufficient for us is Allah, and [He is] the best Disposer of affairs."

    Why it speaks to thisWhen you feel unsure about what lies ahead, these words are a simple way to entrust your journey to Allah.

  7. At-Talaq 65:3

    وَيَرْزُقْهُ مِنْ حَيْثُ لَا يَحْتَسِبُ ۚ وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسْبُهُۥٓ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بَٰلِغُ أَمْرِهِۦ ۚ قَدْ جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ لِكُلِّ شَىْءٍۢ قَدْرًۭا

    Wa yarzuqhu min haisu laa yahtasib; wa many yatawakkal 'alal laahi fahuwa husbuh; innal laaha baalighu amrih; qad ja'alal laahu likulli shai'in qadraa

    And will provide for him from where he does not expect. And whoever relies upon Allah - then He is sufficient for him. Indeed, Allah will accomplish His purpose. Allah has already set for everything a [decreed] extent.

    Why it speaks to thisAs you pack and plan, this ayah reminds you that reliance on Allah is a source of sufficiency.

  8. Taha 20:25

    قَالَ رَبِّ ٱشْرَحْ لِى صَدْرِى

    Qaala Rabbish rah lee sadree

    [Moses] said, "My Lord, expand for me my breast [with assurance]

    Why it speaks to thisIf you feel nervous or hurried before leaving, this short du'a asks Allah for calm and ease.
