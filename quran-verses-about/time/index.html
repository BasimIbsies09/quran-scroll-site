Quran Verses About Time

"By time, indeed, mankind is in loss." These ayahs are about time, its value and how quickly it passes.

  1. Al-'Asr 103:2

    إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ

    Innal insaana lafee khusr

    Indeed, mankind is in loss,

    Why it speaks to thisThis ayah reminds you that time is precious, and your concern about wasting it shows you are taking it seriously.

  2. Ash-Sharh 94:7

    فَإِذَا فَرَغْتَ فَٱنصَبْ

    Fa iza faragh ta fansab

    So when you have finished [your duties], then stand up [for worship].

    Why it speaks to thisIt encourages you to move from one good use of time to another, so your days stay purposeful and full.

  4. Ali 'Imran 3:133

    ۞ وَسَارِعُوٓا۟ إِلَىٰ مَغْفِرَةٍۢ مِّن رَّبِّكُمْ وَجَنَّةٍ عَرْضُهَا ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُ وَٱلْأَرْضُ أُعِدَّتْ لِلْمُتَّقِينَ

    Wa saari'ooo ilaa maghfiratim mir Rabbikum wa Jannatin arduhassamaawaatu wal ardu u'iddat lilmuttaqeen

    And hasten to forgiveness from your Lord and a garden as wide as the heavens and earth, prepared for the righteous

    Why it speaks to thisYou are invited to hurry toward what is good, and today is a fresh moment to start.

  5. Al-Baqarah 2:148

    وَلِكُلٍّۢ وِجْهَةٌ هُوَ مُوَلِّيهَا ۖ فَٱسْتَبِقُوا۟ ٱلْخَيْرَٰتِ ۚ أَيْنَ مَا تَكُونُوا۟ يَأْتِ بِكُمُ ٱللَّهُ جَمِيعًا ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَىْءٍۢ قَدِيرٌۭ

    Wa likullinw wijhatun huwa muwalleehaa fastabiqul khairaat; ayna maa takoonoo yaati bikumullaahu jamee'aa; innal laaha 'alaa kulli shai'in qadeer

    For each [religious following] is a direction toward which it faces. So race to [all that is] good. Wherever you may be, Allah will bring you forth [for judgement] all together. Indeed, Allah is over all things competent.

    Why it speaks to thisThis ayah gives you a direction for your energy: spend your time racing toward goodness, one small step at a time.

  6. Al-Mu'minun 23:115

    أَفَحَسِبْتُمْ أَنَّمَا خَلَقْنَٰكُمْ عَبَثًۭا وَأَنَّكُمْ إِلَيْنَا لَا تُرْجَعُونَ

    Afahsibtum annamaa khalaqnaakum 'abasanw wa annakum ilainaa laa turja'oon

    Then did you think that We created you uselessly and that to Us you would not be returned?"

    Why it speaks to thisYou were not created without purpose, which is a hopeful reminder when your days feel empty.

  7. Adh-Dhariyat 51:56

    وَمَا خَلَقْتُ ٱلْجِنَّ وَٱلْإِنسَ إِلَّا لِيَعْبُدُونِ

    Wa maa khalaqtul jinna wal insa illaa liya'budoon

    And I did not create the jinn and mankind except to worship Me.

    Why it speaks to thisIt points to a clear purpose that can give shape and meaning to your hours.

  8. Ash-Sharh 94:8

    وَإِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ فَٱرْغَب

    Wa ilaa rabbika far ghab

    And to your Lord direct [your] longing.

    Why it speaks to thisYour desire to do better can be turned toward Allah, who knows your intentions and efforts.

  9. Taha 20:114

    فَتَعَٰلَى ٱللَّهُ ٱلْمَلِكُ ٱلْحَقُّ ۗ وَلَا تَعْجَلْ بِٱلْقُرْءَانِ مِن قَبْلِ أَن يُقْضَىٰٓ إِلَيْكَ وَحْيُهُۥ ۖ وَقُل رَّبِّ زِدْنِى عِلْمًۭا

    Fata'aalal laahul Malikul Haqq; wa laa ta'jal bil Quraani min qabli ai yuqdaaa ilaika wahyuhoo wa qur Rabbi zidnee 'ilmaa

    So high [above all] is Allah, the Sovereign, the Truth. And, [O Muhammad], do not hasten with [recitation of] the Qur'an before its revelation is completed to you, and say, "My Lord, increase me in knowledge."

    Why it speaks to thisThis short du'a is one you can make as you look for how to use your time well.
