Questions

Common questions

Is Quran Scroll free?

Yes. Download it free on the App Store. Reading the whole Quran, recitation, streaks and reading plans are free. Quran Scroll Pro adds every deep study, word-by-word meanings and root words, and unlimited AI ayah search.

What’s in Quran Scroll Pro?

Every deep study with word-by-word meanings and root words, context and reflections, unlimited AI search for ayahs that speak to your situation, and AI explanations of why an ayah may speak to you. Reading the Quran itself is always free.

How does the AI find ayahs for my situation?

You describe what you’re going through and an AI model suggests ayahs whose plain meaning speaks to it, with a short note on why. Every suggestion is checked against the Quran text before you see it, and anything that doesn’t match is dropped. What you write isn’t stored. If you mention self-harm or being harmed, we show you where to get help instead.

Which translation does it use?

The Uthmani Arabic text with the Saheeh International English translation and transliteration, plus optional ayah-by-ayah recitation.

Is it on Android?

Quran Scroll is on iPhone and iPad today. You can read every surah on this website from any device in the meantime.

Do I need an account?

No. There’s no sign-up, no email and no password. Your streak, saved ayahs and progress stay on your phone.
