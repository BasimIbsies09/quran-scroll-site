Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Quran Scroll is a Quran reader for iPhone. It has no accounts and no sign-in. Your reading, saved ayahs, plans and settings stay on your device. The only data that leaves your phone is what the ad-measurement and subscription services described below need to work, and ad measurement happens only if you allow tracking when iOS asks.

This policy explains what the Quran Scroll app ("the app", "we", "us") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It applies to the iOS app distributed through the Apple App Store.

1. No account, no sign-in

You do not create an account to use Quran Scroll, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number or password. We do not operate our own servers that store information about you.

2. Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following on your phone so it can remember where you are:

  • Saved ayahs, the ayah you last read, your reading plan and the days you completed, and your reading streak.
  • Settings such as dark mode, the reminder timing you chose, and whether you have finished onboarding.
  • Whether you have an active subscription, mirrored from Apple and Superwall (see below), and whether the app has already asked you for a review.

This information is stored locally in the app's storage. It is not uploaded to us. Deleting the app, or using Reset app in the Menu tab, removes it.

3. Information collected automatically

The app includes software from three companies. Each receives a limited amount of technical data directly from your device.

Meta (Facebook SDK) for ad measurement

We advertise Quran Scroll on Meta's platforms (Facebook and Instagram). To measure whether those ads work, the app includes the Facebook SDK, which sends app events to Meta Platforms, Inc.:

  • Events: that the app was installed and opened, that onboarding was completed, and that a free trial, subscription or one-time purchase started (including the product and price, but never your payment details).
  • Device and app data: IP address, device model, operating system version, app version, language, time zone, and the SDK's own anonymous identifiers.
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA): only if you tap Allow on the iOS "Allow Quran Scroll to track your activity" prompt, which the app shows once during onboarding. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the SDK still reports the events above, but without the advertising identifier, and Meta uses Apple's privacy-preserving SKAdNetwork instead.

Meta uses this data to attribute installs and purchases to ad campaigns and, where you have allowed tracking, for its own advertising purposes as described in the Meta Privacy Policy. We do not send Meta anything about what you read in the Quran, what you save, or your plans.

Superwall for subscriptions

Subscription screens ("paywalls"), free-trial offers and the restore-purchases flow are provided by Superwall (Superwall, Inc.). The Superwall SDK sends Superwall an anonymous app-user identifier it generates, your device model and OS version, app version, language and region, which paywalls you saw, and the outcome of each purchase, trial and restore so your subscription status can be checked. Superwall does not receive your name, email or payment details. See the Superwall Privacy Policy.

Apple

All purchases are made through Apple's App Store using your Apple Account. Apple processes the payment and holds your billing information; we never see your card number. Apple also provides the in-app review prompt and the App Tracking Transparency prompt. Apple's handling of this data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Expo (app updates)

The app is built with Expo and may check Expo's update service (Expo, Inc.) for small over-the-air updates when it launches. That request includes the platform, app and runtime version, and no personal information. See the Expo Privacy Policy.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a heads-up before your free trial ends and a daily reading reminder. These are local notifications created on your device. We do not run a push-notification server and do not collect notification tokens. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • We do not use third-party analytics beyond the services listed above, and we do not show third-party ads inside the app.
  • We do not share what you read, search or save with anyone.

6. Your choices

  • Ad tracking: change your answer at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Quran Scroll. Turning it off stops the advertising identifier from being shared with Meta.
  • Notifications: iOS Settings → Notifications → Quran Scroll.
  • Local data: Menu → Reset app clears saved ayahs, plans and progress; deleting the app removes everything stored on the device.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions, or from Menu → Manage subscription in the app.
  • Data held by Meta, Superwall or Apple: each company's privacy policy linked above explains how to access or delete the data it holds.

7. Retention

Data stored on your device stays until you reset or delete the app. Event data sent to Meta and Superwall is retained by those companies according to their own policies; we do not keep a separate copy.

8. Children

Quran Scroll is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. International users

Meta, Superwall and Apple may process the data described above in the United States and other countries. Where required, those companies rely on their own legal safeguards for international transfers.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's App Store release notes.
