Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Quran Scroll is a Quran reader for iPhone. It has no accounts and no sign-in. Your reading, saved ayahs, plans and settings stay on your device. The only data that leaves your phone is anonymous usage statistics that help us improve the app, and what the ad-measurement, subscription and optional recitation and AI services described below need to work.

This policy explains what the Quran Scroll app ("the app", "we", "us") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It applies to the iOS app distributed through the Apple App Store.

1. No account, no sign-in

You do not create an account to use Quran Scroll, and the app never asks for your name, email address, phone number or password. The only exception is the optional text-message waitlist on our website, described in section 5. Apart from that waitlist and the anonymous usage statistics described in section 3, we do not operate our own servers that store information about you.

2. Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following on your phone so it can remember where you are:

  • Saved ayahs, the ayah you last read, your reading plan and the days you completed, and your reading streak.
  • Settings such as dark mode, the reminder timing you chose, and whether you have finished onboarding.
  • Whether you have an active subscription, mirrored from Apple and Superwall (see below), and whether the app has already asked you for a review.

This information is stored locally in the app's storage. It is not uploaded to us. Deleting the app, or using Reset app in the Menu tab, removes it.

3. Information collected automatically

The app sends anonymous usage statistics to us, and includes software from other companies that each receive a limited amount of technical data directly from your device.

Quran Scroll (anonymous usage statistics)

To learn which features help people keep reading and where the app falls short, the app sends us usage statistics. They are stored on our database host, Supabase (Supabase, Inc.), and are not shared with anyone else.

  • Identifier: a random ID the app creates on first launch and stores on your device. It is not your advertising identifier and is not linked to your name, Apple Account, phone number or any other account. Deleting the app deletes the ID.
  • Usage events: when the app is opened and for how long, which screens are viewed, the onboarding step reached, how many ayahs were scrolled in a session, and feature use such as saving or sharing an ayah, streak days, the daily goal, reading plans, recitation, the widget, notification taps, paywall views and purchase outcomes (never payment details), and settings choices.
  • Device and app data: app version, iOS version, language, time zone, and app errors.

We do not collect which ayahs you read or save, anything you type, your location, or your IP address as part of these statistics (our host necessarily sees the IP address of each request but we do not store it with them). We use this information only to measure retention and improve the app.

Meta (Facebook SDK) for ad measurement

We advertise Quran Scroll on Meta's platforms (Facebook and Instagram). To measure whether those ads work, the app includes the Facebook SDK, which sends app events to Meta Platforms, Inc.:

  • Events: that the app was installed and opened, that onboarding was completed, and that a free trial, subscription or one-time purchase started (including the product and price, but never your payment details).
  • Device and app data: IP address, device model, operating system version, app version, language, time zone, and the SDK's own anonymous identifiers.
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA): only if you tap Allow on the iOS "Allow Quran Scroll to track your activity" prompt, which the app shows once during onboarding. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the SDK still reports the events above, but without the advertising identifier, and Meta uses Apple's privacy-preserving SKAdNetwork instead.

Meta uses this data to attribute installs and purchases to ad campaigns and, where you have allowed tracking, for its own advertising purposes as described in the Meta Privacy Policy. We do not send Meta anything about what you read in the Quran, what you save, or your plans.

Superwall for subscriptions

Subscription screens ("paywalls"), free-trial offers and the restore-purchases flow are provided by Superwall (Superwall, Inc.). The Superwall SDK sends Superwall an anonymous app-user identifier it generates, your device model and OS version, app version, language and region, which paywalls you saw, and the outcome of each purchase, trial and restore so your subscription status can be checked. Superwall does not receive your name, email or payment details. See the Superwall Privacy Policy.

Apple

All purchases are made through Apple's App Store using your Apple Account. Apple processes the payment and holds your billing information; we never see your card number. Apple also provides the in-app review prompt and the App Tracking Transparency prompt. Apple's handling of this data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Expo (app updates)

The app is built with Expo and may check Expo's update service (Expo, Inc.) for small over-the-air updates when it launches. That request includes the platform, app and runtime version, and no personal information. See the Expo Privacy Policy.

Islamic Network (recitation audio)

If you turn on recitation in the reader, the app streams each ayah's audio from the Islamic Network audio service (cdn.islamic.network). Like any web request, this sends that service your IP address and which ayah's audio file is being requested. Recitation is off until you turn it on, and nothing else is sent.

OpenRouter and Anthropic (AI ayah search and explanations)

When you describe what you are going through on Discover, or tap “Why this ayah for me?” (Pro), the app first asks for your permission. If you agree, what you typed (and, for an explanation, the ayah) is sent through our server and OpenRouter, Inc. to Anthropic PBC, whose Claude AI model suggests ayahs and explains why they may speak to you. We do not store this text on our server or link it to you. OpenRouter and Anthropic handle it under their own privacy policies: OpenRouter and Anthropic. Please do not include names or private details. Suggestions and explanations are written by AI, checked against the Quran text in the app, and are not religious rulings. Nothing is sent if you do not use this feature, and messages the app recognizes as being about self-harm or abuse are never sent.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a heads-up before your free trial ends and a daily reading reminder. These are local notifications created on your device. We do not run a push-notification server and do not collect notification tokens. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

5. Website SMS waitlist

Before launch, our website lets you join a waitlist by entering your mobile number. If you join, we store your phone number, the country you selected, how you found the page (for example, an ad or a shared link), the consent wording you agreed to, and the date you signed up. We store this in our database hosted by Supabase, Inc., and we use a text-messaging provider to send the messages.

We use your number only to send you texts about Quran Scroll's launch and your waitlist gift. Message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase. We never sell your number or share it with anyone for their own marketing, and mobile opt-in data is not shared with third parties except the providers that deliver our messages.

We keep your number until you reply STOP or ask us to delete it, and no longer than 12 months after the app launches. To have it deleted, email us at the address in the Contact section.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • What you type into the Discover box is only sent to the AI service after you agree, as described in section 3. If you decline, or the message is about self-harm or abuse, it is matched to themes on your device and never leaves it.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • We do not use third-party analytics beyond the services listed above, and we do not show third-party ads inside the app.
  • We do not share what you read, search or save with anyone. The only exception is the audio request described above, which happens only if you turn on recitation.

7. Your choices

  • Ad tracking: change your answer at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Quran Scroll. Turning it off stops the advertising identifier from being shared with Meta.
  • Notifications: iOS Settings → Notifications → Quran Scroll.
  • Local data: Menu → Reset app clears saved ayahs, plans and progress; deleting the app removes everything stored on the device.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions, or from Menu → Manage subscription in the app.
  • Data held by Meta, Superwall or Apple: each company's privacy policy linked above explains how to access or delete the data it holds.

8. Retention

Data stored on your device stays until you reset or delete the app. Detailed usage events are deleted after about 90 days; a daily record of which days the anonymous ID used the app, and a summary of the current app version and settings, are kept to measure long-term retention. Because the ID is random and not linked to you, we cannot look up your data, but you can contact us with any questions. Event data sent to Meta and Superwall is retained by those companies according to their own policies; we do not keep a separate copy.

9. Children

Quran Scroll is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

10. International users

Supabase, Meta, Superwall and Apple may process the data described above in the United States and other countries. Where required, those companies rely on their own legal safeguards for international transfers.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's App Store release notes.
