Surah At-Talaq · Ayah 3 of 12

Whoever relies upon Allah, He is sufficient for him (Quran 65:3)

وَيَرْزُقْهُ مِنْ حَيْثُ لَا يَحْتَسِبُ ۚ وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسْبُهُۥٓ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ بَٰلِغُ أَمْرِهِۦ ۚ قَدْ جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ لِكُلِّ شَىْءٍۢ قَدْرًۭا

Wa yarzuqhu min haisu laa yahtasib; wa many yatawakkal 'alal laahi fahuwa husbuh; innal laaha baalighu amrih; qad ja'alal laahu likulli shai'in qadraa

And will provide for him from where he does not expect. And whoever relies upon Allah - then He is sufficient for him. Indeed, Allah will accomplish His purpose. Allah has already set for everything a [decreed] extent.

At-Talaq 65:3 · Saheeh International

Meaning of At-Talaq 65:3

Whoever relies on God, He is enough for them. Provision arrives from where you did not calculate, and God has set a measure for everything.

يَتَوَكَّلْ

yatawakkal: rely, entrust

Root و ك ل (w-k-l). Tawakkul is appointing God as your wakil, your agent. It follows effort rather than replacing it.

Context

Appears in the middle of rulings about divorce, a time of real uncertainty about income and future.

Did you know?

The verse promises provision “from where he does not expect,” the exact phrase many Muslims whisper in hard times.

Reflect

What outcome are you gripping too tightly? Do your part, then hand the result over.

When this ayah speaks to you

Quran verses about anxiety

When worries about the future crowd in, this ayah invites you to place your reliance on Allah, who is described as sufficient.

Quran verses about stress

When there is too much to handle, you can place your reliance on Allah, who is enough for you.

Quran verses about overthinking

When you replay situations you cannot change, this ayah invites you to hand them over. Allah is enough for you.

Quran verses about patience (sabr)

When the waiting feels heavy, this reminds you that you can place your reliance on Allah, and He is enough for you.
