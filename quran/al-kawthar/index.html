Surah Al-Kawthar

سُورَةُ الكَوۡثَرِ

Abundance · also spelled Surah Kausar, Al-Kauthar

Surah Al-Kawthar is the shortest surah in the Quran, at three ayahs. It tells the Prophet ﷺ that he has been given al-Kawthar, abundant good, and to pray and sacrifice to his Lord.

Surah Al-Kawthar (Abundance) is surah 108 of the Quran. It has 3 ayahs, was revealed in Makkah, and is found in Juz 30, page 602 of the standard Madani mushaf.

Surah Al-Kawthar in Arabic and English

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

108:1

إِنَّآ أَعْطَيْنَٰكَ ٱلْكَوْثَرَ

Innaa a'taina kal kauthar

Indeed, We have granted you, [O Muhammad], al-Kawthar.

108:2

فَصَلِّ لِرَبِّكَ وَٱنْحَرْ

Fa salli li rabbika wanhar

So pray to your Lord and sacrifice [to Him alone].

108:3

إِنَّ شَانِئَكَ هُوَ ٱلْأَبْتَرُ

Inna shani-aka huwal abtar

Indeed, your enemy is the one cut off.
