Surah An-Nasr

سُورَةُ النَّصۡرِ

Divine Support

Surah An-Nasr, Divine Support, speaks of people entering the religion of Allah in crowds, and calls for praise and seeking forgiveness. It was one of the last surahs revealed.

Surah An-Nasr (Divine Support) is surah 110 of the Quran. It has 3 ayahs, was revealed in Madinah, and is found in Juz 30, page 603 of the standard Madani mushaf.

Surah An-Nasr in Arabic and English

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

110:1

إِذَا جَآءَ نَصْرُ ٱللَّهِ وَٱلْفَتْحُ

Iza jaa-a nas rullahi walfath

When the victory of Allah has come and the conquest,

110:2

وَرَأَيْتَ ٱلنَّاسَ يَدْخُلُونَ فِى دِينِ ٱللَّهِ أَفْوَاجًۭا

Wa ra-aitan naasa yadkhuloona fee deenil laahi afwajah

And you see the people entering into the religion of Allah in multitudes,

110:3

فَسَبِّحْ بِحَمْدِ رَبِّكَ وَٱسْتَغْفِرْهُ ۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ تَوَّابًۢا

Fa sab bih bihamdi rabbika was taghfir, innahu kaana tawwaaba

Then exalt [Him] with praise of your Lord and ask forgiveness of Him. Indeed, He is ever Accepting of repentance.
