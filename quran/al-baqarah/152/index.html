Surah Al-Baqarah · Ayah 152 of 286

Remember Me; I will remember you (Quran 2:152)

فَٱذْكُرُونِىٓ أَذْكُرْكُمْ وَٱشْكُرُوا۟ لِى وَلَا تَكْفُرُونِ

Fazkurooneee azkurkum washkuroo lee wa laa takfuroon

So remember Me; I will remember you. And be grateful to Me and do not deny Me.

Al-Baqarah 2:152 · Saheeh International

Meaning of Al-Baqarah 2:152

A two-way promise: remember Me and I will remember you. Gratitude is the second half, with ingratitude named as its opposite.

فَٱذْكُرُونِىٓ

fadhkuruni: remember Me

Root ذ ك ر (dh-k-r). Dhikr means both remembering and mentioning: keeping God in mind and on the tongue.

Context

Follows the change of the qiblah toward Makkah, a moment that asked the believers to trust and remember.

Did you know?

A hadith qudsi echoes this verse: “If he remembers Me in himself, I remember him in Myself” (Bukhari).

Reflect

What small act of remembrance can you attach to something you already do every day?

When this ayah speaks to you

Quran verses about gratitude

Gratitude is joined with remembering Allah. Turning to Him often can be the path to a thankful heart.

Quran verses about inner peace

Allah promises to remember you when you remember Him. Even a quiet moment of dhikr draws you close.
