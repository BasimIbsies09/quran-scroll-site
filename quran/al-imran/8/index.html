Surah Ali 'Imran · Ayah 8 of 200

Rabbana la tuzigh qulubana (Quran 3:8)

Also known as Dua for a steadfast heart.

رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغْ قُلُوبَنَا بَعْدَ إِذْ هَدَيْتَنَا وَهَبْ لَنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحْمَةً ۚ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلْوَهَّابُ

Rabbanaa laa tuzigh quloobanaa ba'da iz hadaitanaa wa hab lanaa mil ladunka rahmah; innaka antal Wahhaab

[Who say], "Our Lord, let not our hearts deviate after You have guided us and grant us from Yourself mercy. Indeed, You are the Bestower.

Ali 'Imran 3:8 · Saheeh International

Meaning of Ali 'Imran 3:8

A prayer for steadiness: do not let our hearts deviate after You have guided us, and grant us mercy from Yourself.

تُزِغْ

tuzigh: cause to deviate, swerve

Root ز ي غ (z-y-gh). Zaygh is the slow drift of a heart, not a sudden fall. The du‘a asks to be kept from drifting.

Context

Follows a verse about people who chase ambiguous verses to cause discord, a warning against letting curiosity become deviation.

Did you know?

The Prophet ﷺ often prayed, “O Turner of hearts, make my heart firm upon Your religion” (Tirmidhi).

Reflect

Guidance is given, but staying is asked for. Have you asked lately?
