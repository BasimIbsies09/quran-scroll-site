Surah Al-'Asr

سُورَةُ العَصۡرِ

The Declining Day, Epoch · also spelled Surah Asr, Wal Asr, Al-Asr

Surah Al-'Asr is three ayahs long. It swears by time that all of humanity is in loss, except those who believe, do good, and advise one another to truth and to patience.

Surah Al-'Asr (The Declining Day, Epoch) is surah 103 of the Quran. It has 3 ayahs, was revealed in Makkah, and is found in Juz 30, page 601 of the standard Madani mushaf.

Surah Al-'Asr in Arabic and English

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

103:1

وَٱلْعَصْرِ

Wal' asr

By time,

103:2

إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ

Innal insaana lafee khusr

Indeed, mankind is in loss,

103:3

إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ

Il lal lazeena aamanu wa 'amilus saali haati wa tawa saw bil haqqi wa tawa saw bis sabr

Except for those who have believed and done righteous deeds and advised each other to truth and advised each other to patience.
