Surah Al-Hujurat · Ayah 13 of 18

We made you peoples and tribes (Quran 49:13)

يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ إِنَّا خَلَقْنَٰكُم مِّن ذَكَرٍۢ وَأُنثَىٰ وَجَعَلْنَٰكُمْ شُعُوبًۭا وَقَبَآئِلَ لِتَعَارَفُوٓا۟ ۚ إِنَّ أَكْرَمَكُمْ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ أَتْقَىٰكُمْ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلِيمٌ خَبِيرٌۭ

Yaaa ayyuhan naasu innaa khalaqnaakum min zakarinw wa unsaa wa ja'alnaakum shu'oobanw wa qabaaa'ila lita'aarafoo inna akramakum 'indal laahi atqaakum innal laaha 'Aleemun khabeer

O mankind, indeed We have created you from male and female and made you peoples and tribes that you may know one another. Indeed, the most noble of you in the sight of Allah is the most righteous of you. Indeed, Allah is Knowing and Acquainted.

Al-Hujurat 49:13 · Saheeh International

Meaning of Al-Hujurat 49:13

All of humanity shares one origin. Differences of nation and tribe exist so people may know one another. The only rank that matters is taqwa.

لِتَعَارَفُوٓا۟

lita‘arafu: that you may know one another

Root ع ر ف (‘-r-f). The form implies mutual, reciprocal knowing. Diversity is a reason to meet, not to rank.

Context

Al-Hujurat is a surah of manners: it lays down rules against mockery, suspicion and backbiting just before this verse.

Did you know?

The Prophet ﷺ echoed this in his farewell sermon: no Arab has superiority over a non-Arab except by piety.

Reflect

Who is someone different from you that you have never tried to know?

When this ayah speaks to you

Quran verses about love

This ayah speaks of closeness and understanding among people. It shows that knowing one another is part of how bonds of care begin.

Quran verses about humility

Honor is measured by taqwa, not status, lineage, or achievement. This helps you see others as your equals before Allah.
