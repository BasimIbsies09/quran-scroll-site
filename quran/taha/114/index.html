Surah Taha · Ayah 114 of 135

Rabbi zidni ilma (Quran 20:114)

Also known as Dua for knowledge.

فَتَعَٰلَى ٱللَّهُ ٱلْمَلِكُ ٱلْحَقُّ ۗ وَلَا تَعْجَلْ بِٱلْقُرْءَانِ مِن قَبْلِ أَن يُقْضَىٰٓ إِلَيْكَ وَحْيُهُۥ ۖ وَقُل رَّبِّ زِدْنِى عِلْمًۭا

Fata'aalal laahul Malikul Haqq; wa laa ta'jal bil Quraani min qabli ai yuqdaaa ilaika wahyuhoo wa qur Rabbi zidnee 'ilmaa

So high [above all] is Allah, the Sovereign, the Truth. And, [O Muhammad], do not hasten with [recitation of] the Qur'an before its revelation is completed to you, and say, "My Lord, increase me in knowledge."

Taha 20:114 · Saheeh International

Meaning of Taha 20:114

The Prophet ﷺ is taught a du‘a: “My Lord, increase me in knowledge.” It is the only thing the Quran instructs him to ask for more of.

عِلْمًا

‘ilman: knowledge

Root ع ل م (‘-l-m). The same root gives ‘alam, world, and ‘alamah, sign. Knowledge is reading the signs in the world.

Context

Revealed while the Prophet ﷺ was receiving revelation, telling him not to rush ahead of Jibril in reciting.

Did you know?

Of all things, wealth, power, ease, the Quran singles out knowledge as the thing to keep asking for.

Reflect

Say rabbi zidni ‘ilma before you open this app tomorrow.
