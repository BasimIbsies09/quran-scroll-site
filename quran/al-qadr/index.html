Surah Al-Qadr

سُورَةُ القَدۡرِ

The Power, Fate · also spelled Laylat al-Qadr, Surah Qadr

Surah Al-Qadr describes Laylat al-Qadr, the Night of Decree, in which the Quran was first revealed, and says it is "better than a thousand months." Muslims seek it in the last ten nights of Ramadan.

Surah Al-Qadr (The Power, Fate) is surah 97 of the Quran. It has 5 ayahs, was revealed in Makkah, and is found in Juz 30, page 598 of the standard Madani mushaf.

Surah Al-Qadr in Arabic and English

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

97:1

بِّسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ إِنَّآ أَنزَلْنَٰهُ فِى لَيْلَةِ ٱلْقَدْرِ

Innaa anzalnaahu fee lailatil qadr

Indeed, We sent the Qur'an down during the Night of Decree.

97:2

وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا لَيْلَةُ ٱلْقَدْرِ

Wa maa adraaka ma lailatul qadr

And what can make you know what is the Night of Decree?

97:3

لَيْلَةُ ٱلْقَدْرِ خَيْرٌۭ مِّنْ أَلْفِ شَهْرٍۢ

Lailatul qadri khairum min alfee shahr

The Night of Decree is better than a thousand months.

97:4

تَنَزَّلُ ٱلْمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ وَٱلرُّوحُ فِيهَا بِإِذْنِ رَبِّهِم مِّن كُلِّ أَمْرٍۢ

Tanaz zalul malaa-ikatu war roohu feeha bi izni-rab bihim min kulli amr

The angels and the Spirit descend therein by permission of their Lord for every matter.

97:5

سَلَٰمٌ هِىَ حَتَّىٰ مَطْلَعِ ٱلْفَجْرِ

Salaamun hiya hattaa mat la'il fajr

Peace it is until the emergence of dawn.
