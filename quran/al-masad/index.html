Surah Al-Masad

سُورَةُ المَسَدِ

The Palm Fibre · also spelled Al-Lahab, Tabbat

Surah Al-Masad (The Palm Fibre) is surah 111 of the Quran. It has 5 ayahs, was revealed in Makkah, and is found in Juz 30, page 603 of the standard Madani mushaf.

Surah Al-Masad in Arabic and English

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

111:1

تَبَّتْ يَدَآ أَبِى لَهَبٍۢ وَتَبَّ

Tab bat yadaa abee Lahabinw-wa tabb

May the hands of Abu Lahab be ruined, and ruined is he.

111:2

مَآ أَغْنَىٰ عَنْهُ مَالُهُۥ وَمَا كَسَبَ

Maa aghna 'anhu maaluhu wa ma kasab

His wealth will not avail him or that which he gained.

111:3

سَيَصْلَىٰ نَارًۭا ذَاتَ لَهَبٍۢ

Sa yas laa naran zaata lahab

He will [enter to] burn in a Fire of [blazing] flame

111:4

وَٱمْرَأَتُهُۥ حَمَّالَةَ ٱلْحَطَبِ

Wam ra-atuh hamma latal-hatab

And his wife [as well] - the carrier of firewood.

111:5

فِى جِيدِهَا حَبْلٌۭ مِّن مَّسَدٍۭ

Fee jeediha hab lum mim-masad

Around her neck is a rope of [twisted] fiber.
