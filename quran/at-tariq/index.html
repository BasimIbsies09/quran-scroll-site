Surah At-Tariq

سُورَةُ الطَّارِقِ

The Morning Star · also spelled At-Taariq

Surah At-Tariq (The Morning Star) is surah 86 of the Quran. It has 17 ayahs, was revealed in Makkah, and is found in Juz 30, page 591 of the standard Madani mushaf.

Surah At-Tariq in Arabic and English

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

86:1

وَٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلطَّارِقِ

Wassamaaa'i wattaariq

By the sky and the night comer -

86:2

وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلطَّارِقُ

Wa maaa adraaka mattaariq

And what can make you know what is the night comer?

86:3

ٱلنَّجْمُ ٱلثَّاقِبُ

Annajmus saaqib

It is the piercing star -

86:4

إِن كُلُّ نَفْسٍۢ لَّمَّا عَلَيْهَا حَافِظٌۭ

In kullu nafsil lammaa 'alaihaa haafiz

There is no soul but that it has over it a protector.

86:5

فَلْيَنظُرِ ٱلْإِنسَٰنُ مِمَّ خُلِقَ

Fal yanzuril insaanu mimma khuliq

So let man observe from what he was created.

86:6

خُلِقَ مِن مَّآءٍۢ دَافِقٍۢ

Khuliqa mim maaa'in daafiq

He was created from a fluid, ejected,

86:7

يَخْرُجُ مِنۢ بَيْنِ ٱلصُّلْبِ وَٱلتَّرَآئِبِ

Yakhruju mim bainissulbi wat taraaa'ib

Emerging from between the backbone and the ribs.

86:8

إِنَّهُۥ عَلَىٰ رَجْعِهِۦ لَقَادِرٌۭ

Innahoo 'alaa raj'ihee laqaadir

Indeed, Allah, to return him [to life], is Able.

86:9

يَوْمَ تُبْلَى ٱلسَّرَآئِرُ

Yawma tublas saraaa'ir

The Day when secrets will be put on trial,

86:10

فَمَا لَهُۥ مِن قُوَّةٍۢ وَلَا نَاصِرٍۢ

Famaa lahoo min quwwatinw wa laa naasir

Then man will have no power or any helper.

86:11

وَٱلسَّمَآءِ ذَاتِ ٱلرَّجْعِ

Wassamaaa'i zaatir raj'

By the sky which returns [rain]

86:12

وَٱلْأَرْضِ ذَاتِ ٱلصَّدْعِ

Wal ardi zaatis sad'

And [by] the earth which cracks open,

86:13

إِنَّهُۥ لَقَوْلٌۭ فَصْلٌۭ

Innahoo laqawlun fasl

Indeed, the Qur'an is a decisive statement,

86:14

وَمَا هُوَ بِٱلْهَزْلِ

Wa maa huwa bil hazl

And it is not amusement.

86:15

إِنَّهُمْ يَكِيدُونَ كَيْدًۭا

Innahum yakeedoona kaidaa

Indeed, they are planning a plan,

86:16

وَأَكِيدُ كَيْدًۭا

Wa akeedu kaidaa

But I am planning a plan.

86:17

فَمَهِّلِ ٱلْكَٰفِرِينَ أَمْهِلْهُمْ رُوَيْدًۢا

Famahhilil kaafireena amhilhum ruwaidaa

So allow time for the disbelievers. Leave them awhile.
