Surah Az-Zumar · Ayah 53 of 75

Do not despair of the mercy of Allah (Quran 39:53)

۞ قُلْ يَٰعِبَادِىَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَسْرَفُوا۟ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمْ لَا تَقْنَطُوا۟ مِن رَّحْمَةِ ٱللَّهِ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَغْفِرُ ٱلذُّنُوبَ جَمِيعًا ۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلْغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

Qul yaa'ibaadiyal lazeena asrafoo 'alaaa anfusihim laa taqnatoo mirrahmatil laah; innal laaha yaghfiruz zunooba jamee'aa; innahoo Huwal Ghafoorur Raheem

Say, "O My servants who have transgressed against themselves [by sinning], do not despair of the mercy of Allah. Indeed, Allah forgives all sins. Indeed, it is He who is the Forgiving, the Merciful."

Az-Zumar 39:53 · Saheeh International

Meaning of Az-Zumar 39:53

Addressed to those who have “transgressed against themselves,” this verse forbids despair. God forgives all sins; the only door that closes is the one you refuse to walk through.

تَقْنَطُوا۟

taqnatu: despair

Root ق ن ط (q-n-t). Qunut here is the hopelessness that stops a person from returning. The command is: do not do that.

Context

Many commentators link this verse to people who feared their past made them unforgivable, and it was revealed as an open invitation.

Did you know?

Ibn Abbas called it the most hope-giving verse in the Quran.

Reflect

Despair is the one thing this verse tells you not to do. What have you decided is beyond forgiveness?

When this ayah speaks to you

Quran verses about depression and sadness

When hope feels lost, this is a direct call not to give up on Allah's mercy, which remains open to you.

Quran verses about hope

Even when things feel heavy, this ayah invites you not to let go of Allah's mercy. Hope is still open to you.

Quran verses about hardship and ease

Even when you are tired and hope feels thin, this ayah invites you to keep your heart open to Allah's mercy.

Quran verses about forgiveness

Allah addresses those who have transgressed against themselves and tells them not to lose hope. Your past does not place you beyond His mercy.
