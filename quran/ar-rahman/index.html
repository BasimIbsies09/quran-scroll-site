Surah Ar-Rahman

سُورَةُ الرَّحۡمَٰن

The Beneficent · also spelled Ar-Rahmaan, Ar-Rehman, Surah Rahman, The Most Merciful

Surah Ar-Rahman, the Most Merciful, is known for its refrain repeated 31 times: "So which of the favours of your Lord would you deny?" It describes the blessings of creation, the two seas, and the gardens prepared for those who fear standing before their Lord.

Surah Ar-Rahman (The Beneficent) is surah 55 of the Quran. It has 78 ayahs, was revealed in Madinah, and is found in Juz 27, pages 531 to 534 of the standard Madani mushaf.
