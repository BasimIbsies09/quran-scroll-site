Surah Al-Infitar

سُورَةُ الانفِطَارِ

The Cleaving · also spelled Al-Infitaar

Surah Al-Infitar (The Cleaving) is surah 82 of the Quran. It has 19 ayahs, was revealed in Makkah, and is found in Juz 30, page 587 of the standard Madani mushaf.

Surah Al-Infitar in Arabic and English

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

82:1

إِذَا ٱلسَّمَآءُ ٱنفَطَرَتْ

Izas samaaa'un fatarat

When the sky breaks apart

82:2

وَإِذَا ٱلْكَوَاكِبُ ٱنتَثَرَتْ

Wa izal kawaakibun tasarat

And when the stars fall, scattering,

82:3

وَإِذَا ٱلْبِحَارُ فُجِّرَتْ

Wa izal bihaaru fujjirat

And when the seas are erupted

82:4

وَإِذَا ٱلْقُبُورُ بُعْثِرَتْ

Wa izal qubooru bu'sirat

And when the [contents of] graves are scattered,

82:5

عَلِمَتْ نَفْسٌۭ مَّا قَدَّمَتْ وَأَخَّرَتْ

'Alimat nafsum maa qaddamat wa akhkharat

A soul will [then] know what it has put forth and kept back.

82:6

يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلْإِنسَٰنُ مَا غَرَّكَ بِرَبِّكَ ٱلْكَرِيمِ

Yaaa ayyuhal insaaanu maa gharraka bi Rabbikal kareem

O mankind, what has deceived you concerning your Lord, the Generous,

82:7

ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَكَ فَسَوَّىٰكَ فَعَدَلَكَ

Allazee khalaqaka fasaw waaka fa'adalak

Who created you, proportioned you, and balanced you?

82:8

فِىٓ أَىِّ صُورَةٍۢ مَّا شَآءَ رَكَّبَكَ

Feee ayye sooratim maa shaaa'a rakkabak

In whatever form He willed has He assembled you.

82:9

كَلَّا بَلْ تُكَذِّبُونَ بِٱلدِّينِ

Kalla bal tukazziboona bid deen

No! But you deny the Recompense.

82:10

وَإِنَّ عَلَيْكُمْ لَحَٰفِظِينَ

Wa inna 'alaikum lahaa fizeen

And indeed, [appointed] over you are keepers,

82:11

كِرَامًۭا كَٰتِبِينَ

Kiraaman kaatibeen

Noble and recording;

82:12

يَعْلَمُونَ مَا تَفْعَلُونَ

Ya'lamoona ma taf'aloon

They know whatever you do.

82:13

إِنَّ ٱلْأَبْرَارَ لَفِى نَعِيمٍۢ

Innal abraara lafee na'eem

Indeed, the righteous will be in pleasure,

82:14

وَإِنَّ ٱلْفُجَّارَ لَفِى جَحِيمٍۢ

Wa innal fujjaara lafee jaheem

And indeed, the wicked will be in Hellfire.

82:15

يَصْلَوْنَهَا يَوْمَ ٱلدِّينِ

Yaslawnahaa Yawmad Deen

They will [enter to] burn therein on the Day of Recompense,

82:16

وَمَا هُمْ عَنْهَا بِغَآئِبِينَ

Wa maa hum 'anhaa bighaaa 'ibeen

And never therefrom will they be absent.

82:17

وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا يَوْمُ ٱلدِّينِ

Wa maaa adraaka maa Yawmud Deen

And what can make you know what is the Day of Recompense?

82:18

ثُمَّ مَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا يَوْمُ ٱلدِّينِ

Summa maaa adraaka maa Yawmud Deen

Then, what can make you know what is the Day of Recompense?

82:19

يَوْمَ لَا تَمْلِكُ نَفْسٌۭ لِّنَفْسٍۢ شَيْـًۭٔا ۖ وَٱلْأَمْرُ يَوْمَئِذٍۢ لِّلَّهِ

Yawma laa tamliku nafsul linafsin shai'anw walamru yawma'izil lillaah

It is the Day when a soul will not possess for another soul [power to do] a thing; and the command, that Day, is [entirely] with Allah.
