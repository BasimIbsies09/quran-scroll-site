How it works

  1. You describe it. A worry, a decision, a loss, good news: anything, in a sentence or two.
  2. AI suggests ayahs. It picks ayahs whose plain meaning speaks to your situation and writes a short note on why.
  3. We check every one. Each reference is verified against the Saheeh International text. Anything that doesn’t match is dropped.

The notes are reflections to help you connect with the Quran, not tafsir or religious rulings. If you write about wanting to hurt yourself or being hurt by someone, we will show you where to get help instead.
