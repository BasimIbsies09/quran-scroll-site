How many pages a day to finish the Quran?

The standard Madani mushaf has 604 pages, divided into 30 juz of about 20 pages each. So the maths is simple: divide 604 by the number of days you have.

Finishing the Quran in Ramadan

To complete one khatam in Ramadan, read one juz a day, about 20 pages. Split across the five daily prayers, that is four pages after each prayer, roughly eight minutes each time. If Ramadan has 29 days, add about one extra page a day, or read a little more on the weekends.

For two khatams, read two juz (about 40 pages) a day, or eight pages after each prayer. Read more in our guide on how to finish the Quran in Ramadan.
